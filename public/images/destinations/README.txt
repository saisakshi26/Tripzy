Drop your own destination photographs here (landscape, ~1600px wide) and Tripzy will use them automatically:

  maharashtra.jpg   telangana.jpg   kerala.jpg   goa.jpg
  tamilnadu.jpg     meghalaya.jpg   karnataka.jpg   rajasthan.jpg

.jpg, .jpeg, .webp, .png and .avif all work. Use photos you own or that are licensed for your use
(for example from unsplash.com, pexels.com or your own camera). Restart `npm run dev` after adding files.
Without a photo, Tripzy shows its own illustrated landscape for that destination.
